02 / Visual + semantic equivalent

Atomic exchange,
without a blind spot.

The supplied AMX reference is re-expressed as a native, accessible SVG. The surrounding sequence gives agents and readers the identical decision model.

  1. Declare data spaces, read/write sets, and one authority-context snapshot.
  2. Both committees stage their roots and return a participant prepare QC.
  3. The Nexus latch exposes a visible 2-of-2 prepared state.
  4. Payment and delivery move under a single atomic lock.
  5. Commit both legs with a canonical receipt—or restore both roots. No partial state.
